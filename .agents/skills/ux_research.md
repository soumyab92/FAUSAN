# Skill: UX Research for Luxury Haute Couture

## 🎯 Goal
Define user experience patterns, spatial pacing, and interaction heuristics for ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) couture buyers and digital fashion collectors.

## 💎 Principles
1. **Uninterrupted Immersion**: Avoid aggressive popups or distracting banners. Luxury buyers demand clean, calm, cinematic pacing.
2. **Tactile Digital Feedback**: Micro-animations and magnetic button pulls provide tactile satisfaction simulating physical luxury fabric handling.
3. **Effortless Inquiries**: Bespoke fitting requests must be minimal, frictionless, and respectful of collector privacy.
