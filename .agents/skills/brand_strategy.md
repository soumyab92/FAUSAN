# Skill: Luxury Brand Strategy & Editorial Voice

## 🎯 Goal
Codify brand voice, visual tokens, and narrative positioning for the **FAUSAN** digital atelier.

## 🎨 Brand Identity Tokens
- **Primary Obsidian**: `#060608` (Pure void black with high-depth contrast)
- **Atelier Gold**: `#D4AF37` (24K warm metallic gold)
- **Alabaster Silk**: `#F4F1EA` (Subtle off-white luxury primary text)
- **Secondary Silk**: `#A09F9C` (Refined neutral secondary text)

## ✍️ Editorial Tone
- Poetic, confident, understated, and architectural.
- Headings use uppercase serif letter-spaced typography (`Playfair Display` & `Cinzel`).
- Avoid generic retail terms like "Buy Now" or "Sale". Use "Explore Collection", "Bespoke Fitting", "Discover Piece".
