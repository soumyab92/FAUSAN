# Skill: High-Fashion Visual & Asset Generation

## 🎯 Goal
Provide standardized prompt templates and aesthetic constraints for generating high-end luxury fashion imagery and video sequences.

## 📸 Prompt Engineering Matrix
- **Atmosphere**: Studio dramatic rim lighting, obsidian black backdrop, gold highlights, cinematic anamorphic lens, 8K ultra-detailed.
- **Subject**: High-fashion couture model in structural sculpted silk crepe gown with fluid movement.
- **Negative Prompts**: Plastic textures, oversaturated colors, generic backgrounds, low resolution, artifacts.
